How it works

1. Submit a problem & idea

Pick a problem you care about and describe your idea for solving it.

2. Go research it

Before your idea is scored, you have a window to actually dig into the topic — and log what you find.

3. Get your score

Your idea is rated on feasibility, originality, impact, research depth and clarity.

4. Spot your interest

Patterns across the problems you enjoy researching point toward the career paths worth exploring.
